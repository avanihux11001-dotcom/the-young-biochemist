Student researcher, author and founder of The Young Biochemist, exploring the world of biochemistry through research, communication and curiosity.

Introduction

Hello, I am Avani Kaushik.

I am a student passionate about Biochemistry, Molecular Biology, and Scientific Research. Alongside my academic interests, I am a published author, guitarist, basketball player and Karate bronze medalist.

This website serves as a platform to document my independent research, share scientific insights and explore topics in biochemistry. Through research and scientific communication, I hope to contribute to a deeper understanding of the natural world and inspire curiosity in others.

My Vision

I believe curiosity is one of the starting points of scientific discovery. My goal is to continue learning, asking questions, conducting meaningful research and communicating scientific ideas in ways that can be understood and appreciated by others.
