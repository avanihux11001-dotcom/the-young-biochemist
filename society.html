About the Society

The Young Biochemist is a science society for students who are curious about biochemistry and the wider world of science. Members can take part in discussions, debates, projects, and events.

What We Do

Our activities encourage students to explore science, exchange ideas, and learn together.

Science Discussions

Explore questions and discoveries across biochemistry and the life sciences.

Debates

Consider different perspectives on scientific ideas and topics.

Projects

Develop student projects that encourage research, investigation, and science communication.

Events

Take part in science-focused events and activities for students.

Interested in Taking Part?

Students interested in science discussions, debates, projects, or events are welcome to get in touch.
